# Product Roadmap: The Evolution of GuelmaGuide

This roadmap outlines the journey from a local MVP to a nationwide Algerian heritage platform.

## 📍 Phase 1: Guelma MVP (Q2 2026)
- [x] Launch core discovery and community suggestion features.
- [x] Implement point-based gamification for contributors.
- [x] Deploy AI Guide for personalized local travel insights.
- [ ] **Next**: Social sharing integration to allow visitors to share hidden gems on Instagram/WhatsApp.

## 🗺 Phase 2: Regional Expansion (Q3-Q4 2026)
- [ ] **Multi-City Support**: Expand logic to Constantine, Annaba, and Souk Ahras.
- [ ] **Verified Guides**: Official partnership with local licensed tour guides.
- [ ] **Mobile App (PWA)**: Full offline support for hikers in rural Guelma areas.
- [ ] **Community Forums**: Dedicated spaces for local heritage discussions.

## 🚀 Phase 3: Advanced Features & Monetization (2027)
- [ ] **Local Marketplace**: Artisans sell traditional crafts directly through the platform.
- [ ] **AR History Mode**: Use Augmented Reality to overlay ancient Roman ruins over current camera views.
- [ ] **Ad Platform**: Local hotels and thermal baths can boost their visibility via the Partner Ads program.
- [ ] **Digital Souvenirs**: NFT-based loyalty badges for the top 100 contributors of the year.

---

## 💡 How we evolve
Our roadmap is **community-driven**. We prioritize features based on:
1. User feedback submitted via the Community tab.
2. Analytics on most-searched landmark categories.
3. Impact points generated by our most active contributors.
